SolsticeDocs

Your documents. Your machine. Your rules.

A PDF workspace that treats your documents like they belong to you — because they do. Import any PDF, annotate freely, and everything saves locally in a SQLite database on your machine. No cloud sync. No account. No “upload to continue.”
