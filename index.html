The Builder

Tracy Weaver

Founder, Solstice

I build software that runs on your machine, not someone else's cloud.

By day — well, by night — I work at Amazon. By everything else, I'm building Solstice: local-first AI tools, audio plugins, and the infrastructure to prove you don't need a data center to do serious work. My 2012 MacBook Pro has shipped more real software than most startups.

No CS degree. No certificates I didn't earn. My GitHub is my resume — everything I claim, you can read the code for.

I believe software should be yours when you buy it. No subscriptions, no telemetry, no "requires the latest hardware." If it runs on my fourteen-year-old laptop, it'll run on yours.

Cleveland, Ohio. Still building.

People aren't buying in because they like the product. They're buying in because they want to be inside of what we have to offer.
